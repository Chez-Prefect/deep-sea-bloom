About theArtist.

I'm Chez Prefect — an artist based in Juneau. The design mirrors the quiet energy found beneath the surface of the Gastineau Channel, translating the fluid, freezing grace of the deep into a vibrant dance of color.

My work starts with paint and water — the unpredictability of watercolor feels right for something as alive as the ocean. Each piece is original, and when it becomes something you can carry, that means something to me.
